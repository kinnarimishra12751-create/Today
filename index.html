About

I aim to build a versatile, goal-oriented career that puts my skills to work for the institutions I serve, while growing alongside them. Over 11+ years I've taught programming, databases and data mining, guided student projects across web and mobile stacks, and more recently moved into faculty development — training colleagues on AI tools for research, LaTeX, Mentimeter and the Parul University LMS.

As Manager of the ICT Cell, I oversee technology adoption and e-content quality across 30+ institutes, alongside my own PhD research on using multimodal signals to predict depression severity. Outside work, I read, travel, write and design.
